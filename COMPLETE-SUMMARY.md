```
        ↓

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓
        Courier delivery follow-up
```

```
Day -3: Receive beautiful email
        ↓
        See personalized invitation
        ↓
        Save QR code on phone
        ↓
Day 0:  Attend event
        ↓
        Show QR code at exit
        ↓
        Receive goodie bag
        ↓
        Perfect experience! ✨
```

```
Day 0:  Open scanner on phone
        ↓
        Enter PIN (if required)
        ↓
        Tap "SCAN GUEST"
        ↓

        ↓
        See result:
        • GREEN → Hand over bag
        • RED → Already claimed
        • ORANGE → Invalid code
        ↓

        ↓
        Repeat
```
